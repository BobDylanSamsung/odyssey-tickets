FAQs

Getting there

Where is the cinema?
IMAX Sydney is located in Darling Harbour. You can find us at: 1/35 Wheat Road, Sydney, NSW 2000.
Parking
There are a number of secure car parks located in and around Darling Harbour with the closest being First Parking Darling Quarter.
Public Transport

Metro

Alight the Metro at Gadigal Station and continue your path of travel via the Bathurst Street exit. A downhill pathway will take you directly to Darling Quarter. Approx 1.6km.

Train

Take the train to Town Hall Station and exit south into Bathurst Street. A downhill pathway will take you directly to Darling Quarter. Approx 1.2km.

Light Rail

L1 Dulwich Hill Line light rail services run regularly between Central Grand Concourse and Dulwich Hill via Darling Harbour. Alight at Exhibition Centre stop for Tumbalong Park.

Ferry

Catch a ferry to Barangaroo Wharf or Pyrmont Bay Wharf to get within a short distance to the Darling Harbour precinct.

Accessibility

Is IMAX Sydney accessible for customers in wheelchairs or with limited mobility?
Yes. The accessible entry to IMAX Sydney is through the main entry doors on the ground floor. There are lifts situated adjacent to the marketplace on the ground floor, where a team member can assist with access to the Bar (level one) and Cinema (level two).
Wheelchair accessible spaces
Wheelchair accessible spaces with companion seating are available to be booked online. Select from the Wheelchair and Companion spaces on the seating chart when booking your tickets.

General

What are the different seat types?
IMAX Sydney has four seat types including Coca-Cola Private Box, Full Recliner, Couples Recliner and Standard, giving movie-goers the choice to upgrade their experience with multiple seating options. IMAX Sydney also has accessible spaces and seats.
Can all films be shown in IMAX?
Films are specially shot for IMAX. That means not all movies are suitable to utilise the full capabilities of an IMAX screen and technology, but excitedly, there is a great line up planned including blockbusters, documentaries and more. Some content can be adapted for IMAX screens, but no matter what film you watch, you’ll have an incredible cinematic experience from the moment you walk through the door at IMAX Sydney.
What is IMAX with Laser and what are the benefits of this new technology?
IMAX with Laser is IMAX’s most advanced cinema experience, featuring crystal clear laser projection and next generation precision sound, allowing audiences to be immersed into the biggest blockbusters on the biggest screens. The experience is set apart by its 4K laser projection system featuring a new optical engine and a suite of proprietary IMAX technologies that deliver crystal clear IMAX laser projection, increased resolution, deeper contrast as well as the most distinct, exotic colours ever available on-screen. All films presented through the IMAX with Laser system will be digitally re-mastered specifically for the new system to take advantage of the increased sharpness, brightness, contrast, colour and other attributes it can deliver. The soundtracks will also be re-mixed for next generation IMAX precision sound and the multi-channel system.
